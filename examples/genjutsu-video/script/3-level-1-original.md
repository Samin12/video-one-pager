# Script 3: Level 1 — What Genjutsu Is + Your First Edit

All right, level one. Let's start with what Genjutsu actually is.

Genjutsu edits videos you already have, and it does it in two ways.

The first one is Motion Transfer. It keeps the motion, the camera, and the timing from your video, and rebuilds the person, the place, and the whole look from images you give it. Think of it like an actor stepping into a new costume. Same performance, completely different look.

The second one is Object Swap. It changes one thing in your video and keeps everything else exactly the same. It's like swapping one ingredient instead of cooking the whole meal again.

- Genjutsu page: upload a 4–30 second clip + up to 30 reference images → pick Motion Transfer or Object Swap

Now, you can do all of this on the website. But we're going to do it the easy way, by just talking to ChatGPT. And setting it up is kind of like pairing Bluetooth. You connect it once, and you never have to think about it again.

- Download the ChatGPT desktop app

- Add the Higgsfield plugin

- Ask: "Do you have access to Genjutsu?"

Okay, now let's make our first edit. And all you need is a video you already have, plus an image of whatever you want in it.

So here's a clip of me holding this pink bottle. What I want is for the bottle to turn into a magic orb, and after I snap, I want to be wearing this hoodie I'm advertising.

- Give ChatGPT the video + the hoodie image

- Prompt: "Switch the pink bottle to a magic orb, and after I snap, put me in this hoodie."

- Before and after, side by side

And that's it. No reshoot. No editor. No VFX team.

Now, let's talk about doing this cheaply, because this is where a lot of people waste money. You pay per second of video, and the resolution changes the price a lot. 15 seconds at 480p is about 40 credits, so around $2. That same 15 seconds at 1080p is 144 credits, around $7.20.

So the trick is simple. Draft cheap, and only render the one you're keeping in HD.

- Trim the clip to just the moment you need

- Run drafts at 480p

- Render the final at 1080p

- Check the credit estimate before you hit generate

So that's level one. You know what Genjutsu is, you've got it running inside ChatGPT, and you made your first edit without burning through your credits.
