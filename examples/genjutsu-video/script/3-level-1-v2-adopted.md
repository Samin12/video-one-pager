So this is Genjutsu. It's a new tool from Higgsfield, and it edits video you already have. 

And it does that in two ways.

The first one is Motion Transfer. It keeps the motion, the camera, and the timing from your video, and it rebuilds the person, the place, and the whole look from images you give it. Think of it like an actor stepping into a new costume. Same performance, completely different look.

The second one is Object Swap. It changes one thing in your video and keeps everything else exactly the same. It's like swapping one ingredient instead of cooking the whole meal again.

All you give it is a clip, anywhere from 4 to 30 seconds, plus up to 30 images of what you want in it. And you get it back in up to 1080p.

Now, the easiest way to use this is straight from ChatGPT. Once it's connected, you don't have to learn any new buttons. You just tell ChatGPT what you want, and it runs Genjutsu for you. So for that, we're going to need the ChatGPT desktop app.

Now, before you go crazy with this, let's talk about doing it cheaply. You pay per second of video, and the resolution changes the price a lot. 15 seconds at 480p is about 40 credits, so around $2. That same 15 seconds at 1080p is 144 credits, around $7.20.

So the trick is simple. Draft cheap, and only render the one you're keeping in HD.
