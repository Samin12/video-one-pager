## Why this matters

Until now, you won at content and ads by throwing money at it or learning 15 different tools. The problem comes down to 3 things, and Genjutsu hits all 3.

- **Point 1: Every step costs time or money.** Change a prop, an outfit or the person, and you're filming again.
  - Example / story analogy: You finish the shoot, pack everything up, and the client wants a different prop.

- **Point 2: Every step needs a different tool or skill.** Swapping something in a video used to be VFX work.
  - Example / story analogy: This used to take a VFX team and After Effects. Now it's one sentence: "replace the bottle with a magic orb."

- **Point 3: Every new video repeats the process.** Now you can start from what's already working and make the variations in minutes.
  - Example / story analogy: Agencies win on volume. They're testing 30 ads a week while you're still on draft one.

## Level 1: What Genjutsu is + your first edit (cheaply)

- **Point 1: Genjutsu edits video you already have, in 2 ways.** Motion Transfer keeps the motion, camera and timing and rebuilds the person, place and look from your images. Object Swap changes one thing and keeps the rest.
  - Example / story analogy: Motion Transfer is an actor stepping into a new costume. Object Swap is swapping one ingredient instead of cooking the whole meal again.
  - Step by step (screenshare): Genjutsu page → upload a 4–30 second clip + up to 30 reference images → pick Motion Transfer or Object Swap

- **Point 2: Run it by talking to ChatGPT.**
  - Example / story analogy: Like pairing Bluetooth. Connect it once and you never think about it again.
  - Step by step (screenshare): ChatGPT desktop app → add the Higgsfield plugin → test prompt

- **Point 3: First edit: the magic orb.**
  - Example / story analogy: I'm holding a pink bottle. I snap, it's a magic orb, and I'm in a new hoodie.
  - Step by step (screenshare): Upload the clip + hoodie image → "When I talk about the pink bottle, replace it with a magic orb. After I snap, change my t-shirt into this hoodie. Give me back the full clip." → before/after

- **Point 4: Use it cheaply.**
  - Example / story analogy: 15 seconds costs 40 credits (~$2) at 480p and 144 credits (~$7.20) at 1080p. Draft cheap, then only render the keeper in HD.
  - Step by step (screenshare): Trim the clip to just the moment you need (you pay per second) → drafts at 480p → final at 1080p → check the credit estimate before you generate
