# Samin's voice

Taken from his transcripts ("Claude Just Changed the Stock Market Forever!", 1.7M views, and "Claude +
Higgsfield Just Changed Content Creation Forever!") and his own Heptabase drafts. Reuse these lines
nearly word for word; they're his, they're tested, and he reads them comfortably. Swap in the topic.

## How he talks

- Short spoken sentences. He starts them with "So,", "Okay,", "Now,", "And", "Because".
- He checks in with the viewer: "right?", "okay?", "all right?"
- He signposts: "Let me show you how that works." "Now, here's the important part." "That's it."
  "Let's get into it."
- He names the concept right after the example: "...That's a trailing stop."
- He's plain about limits: "I'm not a financial advisor." "Explain where you still review the output."
- Numbers are spoken simply: "$5", "10%", "15 seconds at 480p is about 40 credits".
- He's a little self-aware: "...so you get a massive head start, and I get some subs."
- His drafts are short lines, often one sentence each, with visual cues inline in square brackets:
  "GPT 6 just changed content creation forever, [show montage of cool clips and things from the video]"
- He joins beats with plain connectors ("and that's why", "because") rather than new setups.

## Intro lines

- **Hook:** "[Tool] just changed [thing] forever, and it's because of this new [skill / feature] that lets
  it [capability 1], [capability 2], and [capability 3]."
- **Proof:** "I've actually been using this for the past couple of weeks, and it's completely changed how
  I think about [thing]." / "I've been using it to [...], and [real result]."
- **Levels:** "So, in this video, I'm going to be breaking down how you can get started in three levels.
  First, we're going to get set up. I'll walk you through the basics of [...], and we'll get [tool] and
  all the tech set up. Then, we're going to be building [...]. In level three, we're going to [...]."
- **Objection:** "And even if you've never [done X] before or have zero technical background, you'll be
  able to do this, because all we're going to be doing is simply speaking to [Claude / ChatGPT]. Let's
  get into it."

## Why this matters lines

- **Open:** "Okay, before we get into anything, I need to give you some context on why I'm actually
  making this video right now and why this matters."
- **Authority:** "Because for the first few years of my career, I used to work at JP Morgan, and I
  actually got to see how [...] works up close." / "Because for the past few years, I've been deep in
  the world of content production."
- **Old way:** "Until now, the way you won at [content and ads] was just to throw money at it, or waste
  an insane amount of time learning 15 different tools to get a decent result. And that just changed..."
- **Three things:** "The gap between [them] and [regular people] comes down to just three things. First,
  it's [...]. Second is [...]. Which brings us to number three, which is [...]."
- **The fix:** "It's not just a chatbot helping you write anymore. It's basically your AI video editor."
- **Close:** "You don't need a studio or a big team anymore. The new advantage is knowing how to use these
  tools the right way. And that's what this video is. I'm sharing what I've learned after testing this
  stuff for hundreds of hours, so you get a massive head start, and I get some subs. So let's get into
  level one."

## Level lines

- **Setup:** "All right, let's build this thing. I'm going to be explaining everything as we go, so if
  you're brand new, just follow along."
- **Need-two-things framing:** "If our goal is to get Claude to [X], Claude needs two things: [...] and
  [...]. Let's start with the first one."
- **Bridge to the next level** (he ends levels on what's still missing): "Right now, Claude can trade,
  but it's trading blind. It doesn't know what to buy, when to buy, or why. It's missing the most
  important piece, which is strategy. So, let's get into that." / "Now, this is all great, but the
  thing is, we're still picking stocks ourselves..."
- **Why the rules matter:** "The worst thing you can really do is hand your AI a pile of money and be
  like, 'Go figure it out.'"
- **Recap a level:** "So that's level one. You [did A], [did B], and [did C]."

## Closing lines

- **Next video (CTA END):** "If you want to [make it cheaper / go deeper], watch this video next."
- **Soft close he has used:** "These are just some fun strategies that I've been playing with... I'm
  actually putting together a full course... join my Claude Club."
